# Third-party technology icons

The SVG files in `assets/` are logos of technologies (languages, runtimes,
frameworks, databases, proxies, operating systems…) used by Docker workspaces,
the service catalog and the Architecture view to tell services apart. All
product names, logos and brands are trademarks of their respective owners;
their use here identifies the technology a container runs and does not imply
endorsement. The icon **artwork licenses** are below; the trademark terms of
each owner still apply.

Every file was only machine-optimised with SVGO 4.1 (metadata, comments and
editor attributes removed, ids prefixed `ti-<id>`, numbers rounded to 2
decimals; `viewBox` kept). No logo was redrawn, recoloured (Simple Icons
glyphs are single-colour paths; they get their brand colour from the Simple
Icons data as `fill`), cropped or combined with another one.
